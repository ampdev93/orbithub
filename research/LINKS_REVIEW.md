## Publication gate

The CSV is a research register, **not** website data. To add a link to `content/links.json` confirm it opens in a normal browser, points to genuine Orbit-related material, and fits the existing simple title/url JSON model. Do not add duplicate entries. Keep third-party rights and attribution intact. Social/login-only entries require a manual check.
