About

OrbitHub is a free, non-profit, volunteer-run archival project. The archive is maintained as an open-source labour of preservation and exists solely to document, preserve and provide access to historical material relating to The Orbit.
OrbitHub seeks to preserve material through open, publicly accessible archival platforms wherever practical.

The archive currently holds a significant collection of original source material, including surviving digital tape recordings from Orbit events and original flyer artwork preserved in excellent condition. As time and resources permit, these materials will (hopefully) be digitised, catalogued and made publicly accessible through the archive in order to help preserve the historical record of The Orbit.



Disclaimer

The archive contains original audio recordings, flyer artwork, photographs and other primary-source material. It may also include relevant third-party material where appropriate.
